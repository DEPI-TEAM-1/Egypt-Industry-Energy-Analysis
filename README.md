# Industrial Sustainability Analysis – Egypt (1990–2023)
🧠 Overview

This project evaluates whether industrial growth in Egypt is sustainable by analyzing the relationship between industrial output, energy consumption, and CO2 emissions using real-world data (1990–2023).

🎯 Objectives
Analyze industrial growth trends
Evaluate energy efficiency
Assess environmental impact
Determine sustainability using data-driven metrics

📊 Data
Source: World Bank
Scope: Egypt
Period: 1990–2023
Includes: Industry, Energy, CO2, GDP
🛠 Data Preparation
Data cleaning
Handling missing values
Unpivot transformations
Structuring for analysis

🧱 Data Model
Star Schema
Calendar (Date Dimension)
Fact Tables (Industry, Energy, CO2, GDP)
Single-direction relationships

📈 Key Metrics
Growth (YOY %)
Energy Intensity
Carbon Intensity
CO2 per Energy
Efficiency Ratio
Decoupling (Energy & CO2)
🔍 Analysis

The project compares:

Industry vs Energy
Industry vs CO2

To evaluate:

Efficiency
Environmental impact
Sustainability
💡 Key Findings
Industrial growth exists
Growth is not stable
Energy consumption is increasing
CO2 emissions are increasing
⚖️ Decoupling Insight

Decoupling is used to determine whether growth is independent from energy consumption and emissions.

Positive → Sustainable
Negative → Not sustainable
🧾 Conclusion

Industrial growth in Egypt is not always sustainable, as it often depends on increased energy use and emissions.

🚀 Recommendations
Improve energy efficiency
Use cleaner energy sources
Reduce emissions
